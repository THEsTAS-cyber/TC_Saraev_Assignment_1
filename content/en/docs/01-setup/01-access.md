## How to visit Innopolis VM

1. Open your web browser and type [Innopolis VM](https://vm.innopolis.university) in the address bar.
2. Then you will be redirected to the SSO Innopolis University login page.
   ![SSO Login](../../../en/images/sso.png)
   - If you are immediately redirected to Innopolis VM, don't worry, most likely you are already logged in.
   - If you are not logged in, you will be prompted to enter your Innopolis University credentials (username and password). Then click on the **Sign in** button.
3. You will be redirected to the main page of the website.
   ![Main Page](../../../en/images/main_page.png)
