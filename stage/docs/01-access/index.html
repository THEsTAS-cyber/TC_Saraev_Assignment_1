    How to visit Innopolis VM#

    1. Open your web browser and type Innopolis VM in the address bar.
    2. Then you will be redirected to the SSO Innopolis University login page.
      • If you are immediately redirected to Innopolis VM, don’t worry, most likely you are already logged in.
      • If you are not logged in, you will be prompted to enter your Innopolis University credentials (username and password). Then click on the “Login” button.
    3. You will be redirected to the main page of the website.
